The application evolved into a B2B SaaS platform for dentists. Key requirements included:
-   **Content:** Over 80 detailed post-operative guides extracted from PDFs.
-   **Branding:** DentalRescueBot logo, The OnCall Bot LLC URL, copyright, and dynamic header title (The Dental Rescue Bot Library Of Post-Operative Notes).
-   **Functionality:** Search, PDF download for each guide, professional UI.
-   **SaaS Features:**
    -   Authentication (practice admin, patient logins).
    -   Multi-tenancy for data isolation per practice.
    -   Practice registration, initially envisioned with a 15-day free trial.
    -   Payment integration: evolved from SamCart webhooks, to direct Stripe with delayed billing, and finally back to a simpler SamCart payment with redirect to the app for registration. The flow settled on: WordPress landing -> SamCart payment (slide-out) -> WordPress thank you page -> Registration in app -> Login.

-   **Deployment & Integration:** Guidance on private deployment, integration with  (WordPress), and SamCart for sales/payment.
-   **Admin Management:** Ability to manage memberships, view payment status, reset passwords, and control account activation/deactivation.
