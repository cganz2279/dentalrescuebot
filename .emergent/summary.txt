<product_requirements>
The application's core purpose is to provide post-operative dental care information for patients across various dental specialties. This includes detailed instructions, warning signs, and diet restrictions, extracted from over 80 PDF documents. The application was to be a B2B SaaS platform allowing dentists to manage and provide these notes to their patients.

Key requirements included:
-   **Content**: Over 80 detailed post-operative guides with search capability and PDF download functionality.
-   **Branding**: Integration of DentalRescueBot logo, The OnCall Bot LLC company URL, and copyright notice, with a header title The Dental Rescue Bot Library Of Post-Operative Notes.
-   **SaaS Features**:
    -   Authentication (practice admin, patient logins).
    -   Multi-tenancy for data isolation per practice.
    -   Practice registration, initially envisioned with a 15-day free trial followed by a 9/month subscription.
    -   Payment integration with an existing vendor (SamCart/Stripe). The flow was refined to SamCart payment upfront, followed by app registration, with the app managing ongoing membership.
    -   Admin dashboard for practice management, patient assignment, and data export.
    -   Patient management system to assign specific procedures.
-   **Website Integration**: Integrate with the user's existing WordPress site () for sales and thank you pages, linking to the dental application.
-   **Legal**: Inclusion of disclaimers and privacy statements.
-   **Customization**: Ability for practices to upload logos and customize branding (colors, welcome messages) for patient instructions.
