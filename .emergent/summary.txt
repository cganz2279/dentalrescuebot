<analysis>
The trajectory details the development of a B2B dental post-operative care application, initially an MVP, then expanded with SaaS features. Key challenges revolved around integrating a React/FastAPI/MongoDB stack with a user's existing WordPress site, managing conflicting requirements (patient vs. dentist-only access), and persistent stability issues, particularly concerning URL configuration and authentication flows. The AI engineer progressed through implementing core features, including multi-tenancy, practice registration, and a shift from direct Stripe to SamCart integration. Recurring problems included hardcoded URLs, double login experiences, and a fluctuating understanding of required functionalities, leading to repeated user frustration, refund threats, and calls to support/troubleshoot agents. The AI engineer made several attempts to fix login flows, introduce password visibility, and clarify WordPress integration, but often introduced new issues or reverted previous fixes, especially regarding the backend API endpoint for the WordPress pages. The system transitioned from including patient login functionality to explicitly removing it. Most recently, critical regressions surfaced concerning admin panel navigation and dentist assignment within the practice portal.
</analysis>

<product_requirements>
The application's primary goal is to provide post-operative dental care information, extracted from over 80 PDF documents, to patients via a B2B SaaS platform for dentists. Dentists should be able to manage and assign these notes.

Initial requirements included:
-   **Content:** 80+ post-operative guides with search and PDF download.
-   **Branding:** Integration of DentalRescueBot logo, The OnCall Bot LLC URL, copyright, and a header title The Dental Rescue Bot Library Of Post-Operative Notes.
-   **SaaS Features:** Authentication, multi-tenancy, practice registration (originally with trial), and payment integration (refined to SamCart upfront, app managing membership).
-   **Admin Dashboard:** For practice management, patient assignment, and data export.
-   **Website Integration:** Sales and thank you pages on  linking to the dental app. The crucial clarification later was that login pages (, ) themselves should be hosted on WordPress, not just linking to the app.
-   **Legal:** Disclaimers and privacy statements.
-   **Customization:** Practices can upload logos and customize branding (colors, welcome messages) for patient instructions.
-   **CRITICAL CLARIFICATION:** The app is for **dentists and admin ONLY**, with **NO patient login access**. Patients receive printed post-operative instructions.
</product_requirements>
